--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. FILING PARSER LIMITATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Only the submissions `recent` block is parsed (older `filings.files` shards
    are a documented future extension).
  - Section extraction is best-effort heading-regex, NOT a legal/structural parse;
    a missing section key means "not extracted", not "absent from the filing".
  - companyfacts is not date-filtered, so "latest annual" reflects the newest 10-K.
  - XBRL tag coverage varies by filer/era; unavailable fields are reported as such.
  - HTML->text uses stdlib html.parser (no BeautifulSoup) — readable, not pixel-perfect.
